## Usage Notes

1. **GitHub Profile README**: Place the banner image in the repo root as `banner.png` or `header.png`
2. **Recommended sizes**: 1280x640px (GitHub's recommended banner width)
3. **File naming**: `assets/banner.png` or `images/banner.png`
4. **Upload to README**: `![Aureon](assets/banner.png)` at the top of README.md
